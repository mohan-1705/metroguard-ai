1.0 TECHNICAL METRICS
Simulated train vibration is logged in mm/s (root-mean-square). Elevated vibration indicates bogie misalignment, bearing fatigue, or suspension damping degradation.
- Normal vibration: < 4.0 mm/s
- High vibration: 4.0 - 7.0 mm/s (Requires schedule check within 48 hours)
- Critical vibration: > 7.0 mm/s (Requires immediate trackside bogie inspection)

2.0 ACTION STEPS FOR CRITICAL VIBRATION (> 7.0 mm/s)
- Identify train ID and bogie position.
- Dispatch technician to the next scheduled station (e.g. Ameerpet, Miyapur) for immediate safety audit.
- Inspect suspension mounting bolts, check air springs for leaks, and verify bearing integrity.
- If physical inspection reveals mechanical wear, remove train from simulated service immediately.
