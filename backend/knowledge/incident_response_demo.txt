1.0 INCIDENT SEVERITY LEVEL CLASSIFICATIONS
- NORMAL: No deviation. Log telemetry.
- WARNING: Minor deviation. Schedule next-shift inspect.
- HIGH: Significant deviation. Requires human review within 15 minutes.
- CRITICAL: Extreme deviation. Immediate dashboard alerts, RAG evidence retrieval, and human supervisor approval.

2.0 SAFETY WORKFLOW AUTOMATION
For HIGH and CRITICAL severity classifications:
- No automated actions are allowed without human supervisor approval.
- Once approved, the system generates a Maintenance Work Order, assigns an available technician, and sends a notification.
- Audit trail logging is triggered automatically at each step.
