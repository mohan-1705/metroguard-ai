SOP: BRAKE CALIPER TEMPERATURE EXCEEDANCE (DEMONSTRATION ONLY)
Document ID: SOP-BRAKE-03
Version: 2.5
Last Updated: 2026-03-01
Status: INDEXED

1.0 DESCRIPTION
High brake caliper temperatures arise from continuous brake application, hydraulic valve stickiness, or mechanical caliper drag. Exceeding 120°C introduces brake fade risk.

2.0 TELEMETRY LIMITS
- Normal range: <= 95°C
- High limit: 96°C - 120°C
- Critical limit: > 120°C

3.0 INTERVENTION MANUAL
Upon notification of a critical brake temperature anomaly (> 120°C):
- Alert the simulated driver to monitor braking performance.
- Initiate automated alert to dispatch a maintenance crew to perform a caliper release check.
- Verify brake fluid levels, inspect caliper seals, and check for pad friction wear.
- Maintain speed restriction on the train until temperature falls below 90°C.
