SOP: TRACK TEMPERATURE EXTREMES AND INSPECTION PROCEDURE (DEMONSTRATION ONLY)

1.0 BACKGROUND
Rail track temperatures exceeding 65°C can introduce buckle risk, thermal expansion stress, and rail deformation in standard line structures. This procedure outlines the actions required upon detecting anomalous track temperatures.

2.0 MONITORING METRICS
- Standard Operating Threshold: <= 65°C (Simulated Demo Limit)
- Action Limit (High Priority): > 65°C
- Critical Limit (Immediate Inspection): > 70°C

3.0 CORRECTIVE ACTION PROTOCOL
When track temperature sensors detect readings above 65°C, simulated maintenance controllers must perform the following:
- Verify sensor health and visual alignment.
- Schedule a track safety patrol to inspect the affected track segment for signs of alignment shift or structural wear.
- Reduce maximum simulated train speeds through the sector to 30 km/h until thermal levels normalize or structural checks are complete.
