SOP: AXLE AND JOURNAL BEARING HOT-BOX DETECTION (DEMONSTRATION ONLY)
Document ID: SOP-AXLE-04
Version: 5.0
Last Updated: 2026-05-10
Status: INDEXED

1.0 INTRODUCTION
Journal bearings carry the simulated vehicle weight. Lubricant loss or structural wear causes rapid heat generation ("Hot-Box" condition). Axle temperatures above 100°C constitute a severe risk of wheel lock.

2.0 METRIC CRITERIA
- Target Temperature: <= 80°C
- High Warning Level: 81°C - 100°C
- Critical Alert Level: > 100°C

3.0 EMERGENCY ACTIONS
If axle bearing temperature exceeds 100°C:
- Route the train to the nearest maintenance bay or station pocket track.
- Apply thermal imaging scanner to verify actual journal temperature.
- Top up journal lubricant, check bearing cage alignment, or schedule axle-set replacement.
- Require formal sign-off from maintenance engineer before returning unit to simulated operations.
